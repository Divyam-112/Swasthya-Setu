## Presentation Summary & Core Focus Areas

1. **Problem Statement:** Overburdened public healthcare infrastructure in India, physician documentation fatigue during peak OPD hours, linguistic barriers for rural patients, and lack of guided home rehabilitation.
2. **Proposed Solution:** SwasthyaSetu — an edge-assisted, vernacular clinical intelligence platform bridging rural patient kiosks with hospital doctor workstations.
3. **System Architecture:** Privacy-preserving edge computer vision via browser WebAssembly, multi-tier resilient OCR pipeline (Gemini Vision + OpenRouter fallback), and ABDM-compliant Express/MongoDB backend.
4. **Key Project USPs:**
   - 10 Indian regional languages voice & touch accessibility.
   - Ambient clinical scribe & automatic SOAP synthesis reducing doctor intake time by up to 75%.
   - In-browser real-time posture coaching with zero video streaming bandwidth.
   - Dual-tier OCR extracting medicines, dosages, and diagnostic markers.
5. **Impact:** Scalable, affordable, and low-bandwidth digital healthcare empowerment for underserved rural and semi-urban populations.
