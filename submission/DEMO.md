## Demonstration Walkthrough Overview

The demonstration video highlights the complete end-to-end clinical workflow of SwasthyaSetu:

1. **Patient Kiosk Intake:** Voice-driven conversational symptom assessment across regional Indian languages with automatic risk evaluation and OPD token generation.
2. **Patient Health Workspace:** Daily health routines, medication reminders, biometric nutrition calculations (BMI, calorie goals, macronutrient splits), and complete health timelines.
3. **Computer Vision Pose Correction:** In-browser edge AI pose estimation for prescribed yoga and rehabilitation routines with real-time joint angles and form feedback.
4. **Doctor OPD Clinical Workstation:** Real-time outpatient department queue management, review of bilingual interview transcripts, and synthesized SOAP clinical notes.
5. **Digital Prescription & Records:** Official ABDM digital prescription composition, print/PDF export, and dual-tier medical document OCR.
